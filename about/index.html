Fineboy Producer

I didn’t just want to make music. I wanted to understand how music grows.

I started as a producer. Working with artists eventually made me realize that making great music is only part of the equation.

It started with music.

I started making and recording music around 2015.

At the time, I didn’t have a big platform, major promotion or industry connections. I was simply trying to figure out how to make something I believed in.

My father always emphasized hard work and practice. The idea that stayed with me was simple: practice makes perfect.

Michael Jackson was also a strong influence on how I viewed music and performance. His magazines were around the house, and that early exposure stayed with me.

Music was becoming more than something I enjoyed. I started seeing it as something I could build my life around.

Then I started seeing the real problem.

Music Music
Strategy Strategy

The focus moved from music to strategy.

Around 2021, I decided to take music more seriously.

But the more I worked with artists, the more I noticed something.

A lot of artists weren’t struggling because their music was necessarily bad.

They were struggling because they didn’t have a clear strategy behind the music.

  • They were releasing without knowing what they were building.
  • Promoting without knowing who they were trying to reach.
  • Spending money without knowing what it was supposed to accomplish.
  • Chasing streams without building reasons for people to become fans.

That’s when my focus started changing.

The more I worked, the more I learned.

Strategy before streams.

Too many artists are told to make more music, post more content, run more ads and chase more playlists.

But more activity doesn’t automatically create growth.

You need to know who you’re building for, what you want to be known for, how your music reaches the right people, and what makes those people stay.

That’s why I believe in Strategy Before Streams.
