# Exact words and phrases of independent (mostly Nigerian) music artists, 2025-2026 voice-of-customer bank

Read this first. Quote reliability tiers used below:
- **[ARTIST]** = first-person words from an independent/upcoming artist (strongest VoC).
- **[PUBLIC]** = reaction comments from Nigerian social-media users quoted by Legit.ng (audience voice, not artist voice).
- **[INDUSTRY]** = label/marketing/PR executive speaking (how the trade frames the problem; useful for vocabulary, not for artist pain).
- **[MKTG]** = marketer/blog/competitor copy (shows how the market is already being addressed; NOT customer voice, do not use as testimonials).
- **[NON-NG]** = non-Nigerian.
Every quote below was returned verbatim by a page fetch. Some fetch results were summarised by a fetch tool rather than raw HTML, so spot-check against the live page before putting any quote in published copy, and never present these as testimonials without permission. Dates are the page's publication date unless stated.

Honesty note on volume: I found about 58 verbatim items, but only about 25 are genuine independent-artist first-person words. Raw Reddit, X, YouTube, Facebook and Instagram threads were not retrievable (Reddit was blocked by the fetch allowlist; one Reddit-domain search was rejected by the proxy; X/YouTube/Facebook comments are not indexed by the tools available). The 60-phrase target is therefore met only by counting audience and industry voices, and Pidgin/slang coverage is thin. See Gaps.

## 1. Frustration after release ("nothing happened", "no one is listening")

### Takeaway
Artists describe release as an anticlimax: they drop, then "nothing", with no idea why. The strongest 2025 phrasings are short, flat, almost deadpan sentences about silence. Another frame is quiet shame and disappearing rather than anger.

### Gaps
- No verbatim "my streams are stuck", "I don't know what I'm doing wrong" or "nobody is listening to my music" from an independent artist was found in Nigerian sources. Those phrasings from the brief are plausible but unverified here.

## 7. Self-description words

### Takeaway
Artists call themselves "upcoming" (artist or artiste), "new on board", talented with "God given talents" and a "next African giant". "Independent", "unsigned" and "underground" were not found in artist self-description here. "Struggling" appears only in marketing copy and one 2021 artist line ("I'm struggling with getting a grip..."), about money, not music.

### Cited Findings
- "I'm an upcoming gospel artist" (item 55)
- "new on board" (item 56)
- "God given talents" (item 57)
- "I’m struggling with getting a grip and knowing where my money is and what it is currently doing." (4 Jan 2021, older; money management) — [Zikoko](https://www.zikoko.com/?p=216009)
